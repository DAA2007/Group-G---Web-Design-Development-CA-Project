About Our Project

Our website is dedicated to promoting awareness of UN Sustainable Development Goal 6: Clean Water and sanitation. Access to safe water is a basic human right, yet millions still lack it. Our goal is to educate, inform, and inspire action.

This project was created as part of our Web design & Development module at National College of Ireland. It reflects our commintment to Sustainability, colloboration, and digital communication.

Mission

Our mission is to raise awareness about the importance of clean water and sanitation, and to inspire communities to take action in support of UN Sustainable Development Goal 6.

UN SDG 8 Targets

  1. Universal access to safe and affordable drinking water
  2. Sanitation and hygiene for all
  3. Improve water quality
  4. Ensure fresh water supply and scarcity
  5. Implement integrated water resources management
  6. Protect and restore water-related ecosystem
  7. Expand international coorperatoin and support for developing countries.
  8. Strengthen local community participation in water management
